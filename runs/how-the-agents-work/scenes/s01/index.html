Can a team of AI agents make a more reliable video than one prompt?

This repository splits the job into stages that hand off files.

After intake, one researcher per question gathers sourced facts in parallel.

A scriptwriter cites fact ids for each factual sentence, then trims if the measured audio runs long.

A fact-checker grades every line, scene builders work in parallel, and a tool renders and stitches the video.

Source: Goldman Sachs 10,000 Small Businesses, 2025 · CPA Practice Advisor (reporting Gusto survey), 2025

In a 2025 Goldman Sachs survey, 85 percent of AI users said it raised their productivity.

Gusto found four in five users saw productivity gains of 20 percent or more.

In Gusto's survey, the top barriers were missing expertise and data privacy fears.

So start with one marketing or customer-service task, track the time saved, and expand only if it pays off.

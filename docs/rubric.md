# Video scoring rubric

Owner: Jiaxin. Used to score every test video (key result: average 4 out of 5 or higher). At least two people score each video independently, then compare; if two scores differ by more than 1 point, discuss and agree.

Score each criterion from 1 to 5. Each level has one sentence describing what it looks like, so two people scoring the same video give similar scores.

## Criteria

### 1. Clarity: can a first-time viewer follow and remember the main idea?

| Score | What it looks like |
|---|---|
| 5 | The one idea is obvious by the end of the first scene, and a viewer could repeat it in one sentence afterwards. |
| 4 | The main idea is clear, but one scene is harder to follow than the rest. |
| 3 | The idea comes through, but the viewer has to work for it in several places (jargon, jumps, too much at once). |
| 2 | The viewer can name the topic but not the point. |
| 1 | The video is confusing; the viewer could not say what it was about. |

### 2. Visual quality: do the visuals look professional and help the narration?

| Score | What it looks like |
|---|---|
| 5 | Every scene is clean and readable, matches the narration, and uses the right visual type; nothing overlaps or is cut off. |
| 4 | Looks professional; one or two scenes are plain or a slightly weak match for what is being said. |
| 3 | Readable throughout, but repetitive or generic; visuals rarely add to the narration. |
| 2 | Several scenes have layout problems (crowding, tiny text, overlaps) or visuals unrelated to the narration. |
| 1 | Visuals are broken, missing or distracting. |

### 3. Narration: is the voiceover pleasant, well paced and easy to listen to?

| Score | What it looks like |
|---|---|
| 5 | Sounds natural, well paced, written for the ear; numbers and terms are easy to catch. |
| 4 | Good overall; a few sentences are long or a number is read awkwardly. |
| 3 | Understandable but monotone, rushed or padded in places. |
| 2 | Hard to listen to: frequent mispronunciations, unnatural pauses, or reading that sounds like a document. |
| 1 | Voice is unclear, out of sync, or missing in places. |

### 4. Coherence: does the video work as one story from start to finish?

| Score | What it looks like |
|---|---|
| 5 | Hook, context, main points and takeaway flow in a logical order; every scene earns its place; length is 7 minutes ± 30 seconds. |
| 4 | Clear structure; one scene feels out of place or one transition is abrupt. |
| 3 | The parts are all there but read like separate pieces; or the length is off by 30–60 seconds. |
| 2 | Order is confusing, scenes repeat each other, or the length is off by more than a minute. |
| 1 | No visible structure. |

### 5. Factual accuracy: is everything said true and traceable?

| Score | What it looks like |
|---|---|
| 5 | Every fact traces to a verified source in `03_checked.md`; numbers are stated with their year and place; nothing is stretched. |
| 4 | All facts are true and sourced; one is phrased slightly more broadly than the source supports. |
| 3 | One fact is unsourced, out of date, or overstated, but none is wrong. |
| 2 | One fact is wrong, or several are unsourced or overstated. |
| 1 | Several facts are wrong, or sources are invented. |

Accuracy is checked against `03_checked.md` and, for any doubt, the source page itself. A score of 2 or lower on accuracy means the video fails, whatever its other scores.

# Test prompts

The whole team tests with these same 10 prompts, so results can be compared week to week. Do not edit a prompt once testing has started; add new ones at the end instead.

1. How do electric vehicle batteries work?
2. Why are more companies adopting a four-day work week?
3. What caused the 2008 financial crisis?
4. How to prepare for your first job interview
5. How did Netflix grow from DVD rentals to global streaming?
6. Should cities ban cars from their downtown areas?
7. How does sleep affect memory and learning?
8. What is a large language model, and how does it learn?
9. How does compound interest help you build wealth over time?
10. Why has the Premier League become the world's most-watched football league?

Is generative AI paying off for small businesses?

In 2025, 58 percent of small businesses told the US Chamber they use generative AI.

Census data put it lower: about 18 percent of all firms used any AI at the end of 2025.

Among owners using AI, Bank of America found marketing leads, followed by content and customer service.

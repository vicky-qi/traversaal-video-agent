Turning a single prompt into a seven-minute video is too much to do in one pass.

So our agent breaks the work into short scenes, and handles each one in three steps.

First, it researches the topic, and checks every claim against a source.

Next, it writes the narration for each scene, and turns it into a voiceover.

Finally, it builds visuals timed to that voice, and stitches every scene into one finished video.
